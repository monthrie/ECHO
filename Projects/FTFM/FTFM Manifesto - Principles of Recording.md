## Core Principles

- No Normative Judgments: We do not label actions or individuals as 'good' or 'bad'; instead, we document what happened (e.g., 'this person did this thing').
- Evidence-Based Recording: We include reasons for believing the recorded information is true, assessing the certainty of our records.
- Sophisticated Storage: Information is stored in a way that allows retrieval at a later date, such as timelines of how someone has discussed or voted on a topic.
- Reputable Sources: If reputable sources report on a person or event, it is likely to be documented by FTFM.
- No Claim to Absolute Truth: We do not claim to provide a single source of truth, only a record of events and the likelihood of accuracy.
- Selective Recording: Decisions are made on what is worth recording, acknowledging the possibility of errors in assessing certainty.
