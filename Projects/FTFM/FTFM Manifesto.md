## Core Principles

- No Normative Judgments: We do not label actions or individuals as good or bad; we simply record what has happened.
- Factual Recording: Document events and actions as they occur, without editorializing.
- Likelihood of Truth: Assess and record the degree of certainty regarding the accuracy of the information.
- Sophisticated Storage: Store data in a way that allows for retrieval and analysis, such as creating timelines of topics or votes.
- Reputable Sources: Include information from credible sources to ensure reliability.
- No Claim to Absolute Truth: Acknowledge that the project provides a record of events and their likelihood of accuracy, not a single source of truth.
- Selective Recording: Determine what is worth documenting based on relevance and significance.
