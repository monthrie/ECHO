## Core Principles

- **Non-Judgmental Recording**: We do not make normative judgments; we record only what has happened (e.g., 'this person did this thing' rather than 'the bad man did a bad thing').
- **Evidence-Based Confidence**: We document the degree of certainty regarding the accuracy of recorded events, explaining why we believe something to be true.
- **Sophisticated Storage**: Information is stored in a way that allows sophisticated retrieval, such as timelines of how someone has spoken or voted on a topic.
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: If reputable sources report on a person or event, it is likely to be recorded by FTFM.
- **No Claim to Absolute Truth**: We do not claim to provide a single source of truth, but rather a record of events with an assessment of how likely it is that we are correct.
- **Selective Recording**: Judgments are made only on what is worth recording, acknowledging that errors in certainty assessments are possible.
