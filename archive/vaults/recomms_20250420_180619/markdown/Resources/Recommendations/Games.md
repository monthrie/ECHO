## Overview

This file contains a curated list of game recommendations for personal reference. These games offer diverse experiences from relaxing to intellectually stimulating. Consider setting aside time to try each one.

## Recommended Games

### Hades
- **Description**: A roguelike masterpiece featuring tight combat, incredible character work, and Greek mythology brought brilliantly to life.
- **Notes**: Highly engaging for fans of action games with narrative depth.

### Outer Wilds
- **Description**: A unique space exploration game built around curiosity and discovery, featuring an unforgettable time loop mechanic.
- **Notes**: Perfect for players who enjoy narrative-driven exploration experiences.

### Celeste
- **Description**: A challenging platformer with gorgeous pixel art that weaves mental health themes into both story and gameplay.
- **Notes**: A meaningful game for those interested in stories about personal struggles.

### Stardew Valley
- **Description**: Addictive farming simulator, perfect for relaxing and charming gameplay.
- **Notes**: Ideal for unwinding after a busy day.

### Disco Elysium
- **Description**: Unique detective RPG with phenomenal writing, blending philosophy and humor.
- **Notes**: Great for players who enjoy deep narrative and character-driven stories.

### Hollow Knight
- **Description**: Gorgeous metroidvania with challenging combat and an atmospheric world that encourages exploration.
- **Notes**: Recommended for fans of exploration and challenging gameplay.
