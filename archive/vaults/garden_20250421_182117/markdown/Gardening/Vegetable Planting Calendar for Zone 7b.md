## Planting Schedule
- [ ] Tomatoes: Start seeds indoors in late February to early March, transplant outdoors in late April to early May.
- [ ] Lettuce: Direct sow seeds in early spring (March-April) or fall (August-September).
- [ ] Carrots: Direct sow seeds from March to June for a continuous harvest.
- [ ] Beans: Direct sow seeds after the last frost, typically mid to late April.
- [ ] Cucumbers: Start seeds indoors in early April or direct sow in late April to early May.

**Note:** Adjust planting dates based on exact weather conditions and last frost date (typically mid-April in Zone 7b).
