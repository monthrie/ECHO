## Garden Layout
Plan for a raised bed of 4 feet by 8 feet, a recommended size for beginners. The chosen location receives about 6 hours of sunlight daily, which is sufficient for most vegetables (ideal range is 6-8 hours). Confirming sunlight adequacy for specific crops is advised.

## Supplies Needed
- [ ] Soil (potting mix or garden soil suitable for vegetables)
- [ ] Containers or materials for a raised bed (wood, brackets, etc.)
- [ ] Seeds or starter plants (tomatoes, lettuce, and others suitable for Zone 7b)
- [ ] Basic gardening tools (shovel, trowel, gloves, watering can)
- [ ] Watering system (consider a simple drip irrigation setup for consistent watering)
- [ ] Fencing or natural pest deterrents to protect against rabbits and other pests

## Planting Considerations
Focus on beginner-friendly vegetables like tomatoes and lettuce. Research companion planting, such as planting basil near tomatoes to enhance growth and deter pests. Ensure the garden is ready for planting by early May. Vegetables suitable for Zone 7b include tomatoes, lettuce, carrots, beans, and cucumbers, which can thrive in the region's mild winters and warm summers.

## Pest Management
Consider fencing to protect against rabbits. Natural pest deterrents include companion planting (e.g., marigolds to repel insects), using row covers, and encouraging beneficial insects like ladybugs. Avoid chemical pesticides to maintain an organic garden.
