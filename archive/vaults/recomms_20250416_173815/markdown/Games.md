## Game Recommendations

This file contains a curated list of game recommendations for personal reference.

### Stardew Valley
- **Description**: An addictive farming simulator that's perfect for relaxation with its charming aesthetics.
- **Notes**: Ideal for downtime; consider multiplayer mode for social gaming.
- **Related**: [[Game Rec: Disco Elysium]], [[Game Rec: Hollow Knight]]

### Disco Elysium
- **Description**: A unique detective RPG with phenomenal writing, blending philosophy and humor.
- **Notes**: Highly recommended for fans of deep narrative experiences.
- **Related**: [[Game Rec: Stardew Valley]], [[Game Rec: Hollow Knight]]

### Hollow Knight
- **Description**: A gorgeous metroidvania with challenging combat and an atmospheric world that invites exploration.
- **Notes**: Perfect for players who enjoy challenging gameplay and intricate worlds.
- **Related**: [[Game Rec: Stardew Valley]], [[Game Rec: Disco Elysium]]

### Hades
- **Description**: Roguelike masterpiece with tight combat, incredible character work, and Greek mythology brought brilliantly to life.
- **Notes**: Suitable for both casual and dedicated gaming sessions.

### Outer Wilds
- **Description**: Unique space exploration game built around curiosity and discovery with an unforgettable time loop mechanic.
- **Notes**: Suitable for both casual and dedicated gaming sessions.

### Celeste
- **Description**: Challenging platformer with gorgeous pixel art that weaves mental health themes into both story and gameplay.
- **Notes**: Suitable for both casual and dedicated gaming sessions.
