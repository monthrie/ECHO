## Film Recommendations

This file contains a curated list of film recommendations for personal reference.

### Everything Everywhere All at Once
- **Description**: A mind-bending multiverse adventure with surprising emotional depth.
- **Notes**: Great for fans of imaginative storytelling and genre-blending.
- **Related**: [[Film Rec: Parasite]], [[Film Rec: Arrival]]

### Parasite
- **Description**: Bong Joon-ho's masterpiece that brilliantly shifts genres while delivering sharp social commentary.
- **Notes**: Explore other works by Bong Joon-ho for similar thematic explorations.
- **Related**: [[Film Rec: Everything Everywhere All at Once]], [[Film Rec: Arrival]]

### Arrival
- **Description**: Thoughtful sci-fi focusing on language and communication rather than typical alien invasion tropes.
- **Notes**: Might inspire interest in linguistics and Sci-Fi storytelling.
- **Related**: [[Film Rec: Everything Everywhere All at Once]], [[Film Rec: Parasite]]

### The Grand Budapest Hotel by Wes Anderson
- **Description**: Visually stunning comedy with incredible attention to detail and a surprising emotional core.
- **Notes**: Perfect for movie nights or thematic analysis.

### Drive My Car
- **Description**: Beautifully patient Japanese drama about grief, art, and human connection that stays with you.
- **Notes**: Perfect for movie nights or thematic analysis.

### Portrait of a Lady on Fire
- **Description**: Exquisite period romance with breathtaking cinematography and powerful performances.
- **Notes**: Perfect for movie nights or thematic analysis.
