This note provides detailed profiles of beginner-friendly vegetables suitable for Zone 7b.

## Vegetable Profiles
- **Content**: Profiles includes Lettuce (grows quickly, harvest in 30-60 days, plant in spring/fall), Radishes (fastest growing, harvest in 20-30 days, cool weather), Carrots (hardy, harvest in 60-80 days, needs loose soil), Green Beans (prolific, harvest in 50-60 days, plant post-frost), Zucchini (vigorous, harvest in 40-50 days, full sun), Tomatoes (versatile, harvest in 60-80 days, needs staking), Spinach (cold-hardy, harvest in 40-50 days, spring/fall). Includes planting tips, varieties, and challenges.
- **Category**: Vegetable Gardening
- **Format**: Reference Guide
- **Notes**: Use this as a reference for selecting vegetables to grow. Consider creating individual planting schedules based on specific varieties and local conditions.
