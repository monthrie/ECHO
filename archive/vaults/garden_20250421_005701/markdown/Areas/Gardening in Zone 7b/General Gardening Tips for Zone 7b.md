This note offers practical gardening tips for beginners in Zone 7b.

## Tips
- **Content**: Know frost dates (mid-April last frost, mid-October first frost), prepare soil (pH 6.0-7.0, add compost), ensure consistent watering (1 inch/week), use mulch for moisture retention, manage pests with resistant varieties and crop rotation, and extend seasons with row covers or cold frames.
- **Category**: Gardening Practices
- **Format**: Guide
- **Notes**: These tips provide a practical foundation for starting a garden. Consider integrating into a seasonal checklist for ongoing reference.
