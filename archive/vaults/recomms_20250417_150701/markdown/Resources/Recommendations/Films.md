These films offer a range of genres and emotional depth, perfect for movie nights or thematic analysis.

## Recommended Films
- **The Grand Budapest Hotel by Wes Anderson**: Visually stunning comedy with incredible attention to detail and a surprising emotional core.
- **Drive My Car**: Beautifully patient Japanese drama about grief, art, and human connection that stays with you.
- **Portrait of a Lady on Fire**: Exquisite period romance with breathtaking cinematography and powerful performances.
