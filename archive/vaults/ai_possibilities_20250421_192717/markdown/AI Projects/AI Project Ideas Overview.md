## Overview

This note captures a brainstorming session on various AI projects with potential professional impact. The focus is on identifying key opportunities and directions in the AI space for collaboration or independent development.

## Project Ideas

- **Human Rights Due Diligence Tool with Dan Murphy**: Exploring possibilities with Dan Murphy to raise money for a Human Rights due diligence tool. Options include selling or structuring Dan's data, or turning it into a model for fine-tuning AI. Potential collaboration with CLIM.
  
- **Enhancing Travis's Social Media Presence**: Working with Travis to grow his 200,000 Instagram followers, monetize his audience, or create a team of AI agents to support his work. This could evolve into a sellable business or product, potentially advertised by Travis.
  
- **VITA: Autonomous Digital Life Form**: Building VITA as the first autonomous digital life form. Additional ideas include creating an agent arena and seeking funding from early minima investors.
  
- **AI Second Brain Development**: Identifying a market gap for an AI second brain. Noting Open AI's plans for expensive PhD-level assistants, the opportunity lies in creating powerful agent frameworks. Potential to use Obsidian as a frontend, possibly a custom version. Requires a strong proof of concept to pitch effectively.
  
- **Follow the Fucking Money (FTFM) Platform**: Developing FTFM as a platform or Substack to map world connections. Options include open-source contributions with personal API keys, running a free service on AWS, or using open models via Open Router or Mistral. Inspired by Glenn Greenwald's approach to highlight inconsistencies in podcasts and articles, creating a visual map of global connections.
