## Concept Overview
A brief note on a new category for personal journaling called 'Daily Story' and 'Weekly Story', intended to capture short, memorable moments or events in a sentence or two for personal record-keeping.

## Daily and Weekly Story Details
A new database category for journaling:
- **Daily Story**: Captures a single sentence about a memorable moment (e.g., 'Cool with Ralphie').
- **Weekly Story**: Summarizes a notable event of the week (e.g., 'Memorable video call with Jaz').
