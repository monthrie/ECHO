## Background and Assets
Over the past 18 months, extensive research has revealed evidence of forced labor in the supply chains of dozens of brands. A unique methodology for identifying these issues has been developed, with potential for AI automation to replace the current manual process. The dataset and methodology represent significant value for brands and supply chain due diligence firms.

## Strategic Options
1. **Direct Brand Approach**: Contact brands directly with evidence of supply chain risks, offering valuable information to prevent penalties under regulations like the UFLBA.
2. **Partnership with Due Diligence Firms**: Collaborate with established supply chain due diligence firms to provide detailed evidence as an additional service for their existing clients.
3. **Data Sale**: Sell the current dataset to a due diligence firm to generate quick revenue and potentially fund further development.
4. **Raise Investment**: Explore investment opportunities, though this is less realistic without a fully developed product.
5. **Equity Investment or Partnership**: Consider partial equity investments or strategic partnerships with due diligence firms as part of payment or collaboration agreements.

## Constraints and Considerations
Limited financial resources restrict the ability to scale operations or build a comprehensive product. While an AI engineer expert is on the team, a larger team would be required for full development. Competitive dynamics are also a concern, as large corporate firms may prefer consolidated services over niche offerings, potentially pushing smaller players out of the market.
