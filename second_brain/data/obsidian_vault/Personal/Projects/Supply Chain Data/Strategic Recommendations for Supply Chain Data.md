## Recommended Approach
Given the unique dataset and methodology, the most viable initial strategy is to pursue partnerships with established supply chain due diligence firms. This approach allows for leveraging their existing client base and infrastructure while proving the value of the detailed evidence and methodology. It minimizes direct competition with larger firms offering broader services and provides a pathway to quick revenue through data sharing or service agreements.

## Secondary Option
As a secondary option, consider a direct data sale to a due diligence firm. This would provide immediate funds to support further development, such as hiring additional team members or advancing AI automation. If structured strategically, this could also include partial equity arrangements to maintain a stake in future growth.

## Long-Term Consideration
In the longer term, focus on automating the methodology using AI to create a scalable product. This could position the company as a unique player in the market, potentially attracting investment or acquisition interest. Avoid direct competition with larger firms by carving out a niche focused on highly specific, evidence-based forced labor data.
