## Core Principles

- **Non-Judgmental Recording**: Only document what has happened without labeling actions as good or bad. For example, record that 'this person did this thing' rather than 'the bad man did a bad thing'.
- **Confidence Assessment**: Include the degree of certainty about the accuracy of recorded events.
- **Sophisticated Storage**: Store information in a way that allows retrieval of detailed timelines, such as how someone has talked or voted on a topic.
- **Reputable Sources**: If reputable sources have reported on a subject, it is likely to be documented by FTFM.
- **No Claim to Absolute Truth**: FTFM does not claim to provide a single source of truth, only a record of events and the likelihood of their accuracy.
- **Focus on Relevance**: Judgments are limited to deciding what is worth recording.
