## Introduction
Having acquired a 5L container of high-quality olive oil, the goal is to use it in dishes where oil is a prominent ingredient. Below are some meal ideas and preparations that celebrate the flavor and richness of olive oil.

## Recipe Ideas
- **Bread and Olive Oil Dip**: A simple appetizer where chunks of crusty bread are dipped in a bowl of olive oil seasoned with salt, pepper, and herbs like rosemary or thyme. Add a splash of balsamic vinegar for complexity.
- **Homemade Chili Oil**: Infuse olive oil with dried chili flakes, garlic, and a bay leaf over low heat. Use as a spicy drizzle over pizza, pasta, or grilled vegetables. This can use a significant amount of oil and be stored for later use.
- **Olive Oil Roasted Vegetables**: Toss vegetables like potatoes, carrots, and zucchini in a generous amount of olive oil, season with salt and herbs, and roast until golden. The oil enhances flavor and texture.
- **Classic Italian Pasta Aglio e Olio**: Spaghetti tossed with garlic sautéed in a large quantity of olive oil, finished with parsley and red pepper flakes. Simple yet oil-centric.
- **Olive Oil Cake**: A dessert option where olive oil replaces butter, lending a fruity note and moist texture to the cake. Recipes often call for a cup or more of oil.

## Tips
- Use olive oil as a finishing oil to preserve its flavor; drizzle over soups, salads, or grilled meats just before serving.
- Store olive oil in a cool, dark place to maintain its quality over time.
