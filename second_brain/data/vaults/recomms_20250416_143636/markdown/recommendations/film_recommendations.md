## Parasite - Film Recommendation

Parasite, Bong Joon-ho's masterpiece, brilliantly shifts genres while delivering sharp social commentary.

**Notes:** A poor family, the Kims, con their way into becoming the servants of a rich family, the Parks. But their easy life gets complicated when their deception is threatened with exposure.

## Arrival - Film Recommendation

Arrival is thoughtful science fiction that focuses on language and communication rather than typical alien invasion scenarios.

**Notes:** When mysterious spacecraft touch down across the globe, an elite team led by linguist Louise Banks is brought together to investigate.

**Tags:** #films #science-fiction #linguistics #recommendation
