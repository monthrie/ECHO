## Pachinko - Book Recommendation

Pachinko by Min Jin Lee is a captivating multi-generational Korean family saga set in Japan.

**Notes:** Epic historical novel that follows four generations of a Korean immigrant family in Japan.

## Dune - Book Recommendation

Dune by Frank Herbert features incredible worldbuilding and lives up to its status as a classic science fiction novel.

**Notes:** Set in a distant future amid a feudal interstellar society, Dune tells the story of young Paul Atreides as he and his family accept control of the desert planet Arrakis.

**Tags:** #books #science-fiction #worldbuilding #recommendation
