# FTFM as Society's Storage Layer

## Conceptual Foundation

The FTFM system would function as society's 'storage layer' for tracking real-world events and statements - something currently missing from our information ecosystem. Unlike Wikipedia, which focuses on static, historical information, this system would track dynamic, evolving narratives and statements made by public figures.

## The Information Gap

Our current information ecosystem has several components:

- **News media**: Reports on current events but lacks systematic storage and retrieval
- **Wikipedia**: Provides historical information but avoids current controversies and dynamic content
- **Social media**: Contains real-time discussion but is ephemeral and unstructured
- **Academic research**: Rigorous but slow and often inaccessible

What's missing is a systematic way to track statements, promises, and claims as they evolve over time, with proper attribution and verification.

## FTFM's Unique Position

FTFM would fill this gap by:

1. **Capturing dynamic information**: Recording statements as they happen
2. **Structuring the data**: Converting natural language into structured, queryable formats
3. **Tracking evolution**: Following how positions and narratives change over time
4. **Providing context**: Connecting related statements and identifying patterns
5. **Ensuring accessibility**: Making this information available in both human and machine-readable formats

## Philosophical Implications

This system raises important questions about:

- The nature of truth and how it's established
- The role of record-keeping in democratic societies
- Accountability mechanisms for public figures
- The relationship between media, public discourse, and governance
- How collective memory is formed and maintained

## Long-term Vision

In the long term, FTFM could become an essential infrastructure component for:

- Journalists researching stories
- Citizens evaluating political candidates
- Researchers studying media and political trends
- Educators teaching critical thinking and media literacy
- AI systems needing grounded, factual information
