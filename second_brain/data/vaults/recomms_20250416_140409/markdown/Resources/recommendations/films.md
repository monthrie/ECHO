# Film Recommendations

A collection of recommended films with brief descriptions and personal notes.

## Everything Everywhere All at Once

A mind-bending multiverse film with surprising emotional depth.

**Notes:** This genre-defying film balances absurdist comedy, action, and existential themes while delivering a touching story about family and finding meaning.

**Tags:** #multiverse #sci-fi #drama

---

## Parasite by Bong Joon-ho

A masterpiece that brilliantly shifts genres while delivering sharp social commentary.

**Notes:** This Oscar-winning film seamlessly blends dark comedy, suspense, and drama to examine class inequality in modern society.

**Tags:** #korean-cinema #social-commentary #thriller

---

## Arrival

Thoughtful science fiction that focuses on language and communication rather than typical alien invasion tropes.

**Notes:** Based on a short story by Ted Chiang, this film explores how language shapes perception and offers a unique take on first contact narratives.

**Tags:** #science-fiction #linguistics #alien-contact
