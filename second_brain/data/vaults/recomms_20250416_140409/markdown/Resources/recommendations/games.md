# Game Recommendations

A collection of recommended video games with brief descriptions and personal notes.

## Stardew Valley

An addictive farming simulation game that's perfect for relaxing with its charming atmosphere.

**Notes:** This indie game offers a satisfying loop of farming, relationship building, and exploration with a deeper narrative about escaping corporate life.

**Tags:** #farming-sim #relaxing #indie-game

---

## Disco Elysium

A unique detective RPG with phenomenal writing that balances philosophical depth and humor.

**Notes:** This text-heavy RPG reimagines the detective genre through a politically charged world with innovative skill systems and exceptional dialogue options.

**Tags:** #RPG #detective #narrative

---

## Hollow Knight

A gorgeous metroidvania with challenging combat and an atmospheric world that encourages exploration.

**Notes:** This hand-drawn adventure combines tight gameplay mechanics with environmental storytelling in a haunting insect kingdom full of secrets.

**Tags:** #metroidvania #indie-game #challenging
