# Book Recommendations

A collection of recommended books with brief descriptions and personal notes.

## The Road by Cormac McCarthy

A powerful post-apocalyptic story with sparse but beautiful writing.

**Notes:** McCarthy's minimalist style creates a stark, haunting atmosphere that perfectly complements the post-apocalyptic setting.

**Tags:** #fiction #post-apocalyptic #literature

---

## Pachinko by Min Jin Lee

A multi-generational Korean family saga set in Japan that is completely captivating.

**Notes:** This epic historical novel follows four generations of a Korean family through their struggles and triumphs in 20th century Japan.

**Tags:** #fiction #historical-fiction #korean #japanese #family-saga

---

## Dune by Frank Herbert

Features incredible worldbuilding that lives up to its classic status.

**Notes:** Herbert's rich universe blends politics, religion, ecology, and technology in a way that established new standards for science fiction worldbuilding.

**Tags:** #science-fiction #classic #worldbuilding
