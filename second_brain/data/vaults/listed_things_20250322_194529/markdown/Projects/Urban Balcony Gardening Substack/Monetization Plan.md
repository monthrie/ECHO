# Monetization Plan

Monetization strategy for the Urban Balcony Gardening Substack will initially focus on building audience with free content, then introducing paid subscription tiers offering premium content such as detailed guides and personalized gardening advice. The approach prioritizes organic growth and relationship building before monetization, ensuring the publication delivers authentic value.
