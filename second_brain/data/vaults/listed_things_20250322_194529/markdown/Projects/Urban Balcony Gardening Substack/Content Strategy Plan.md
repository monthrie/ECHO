# Content Strategy Plan

The content strategy for the Urban Balcony Gardening Substack will begin with free articles covering fundamentals like container selection, soil requirements, and plant recommendations suitable for balcony gardens. Content will include personal photographs of the gardening setup to demonstrate possibilities in limited spaces. Publishing cadence will be weekly with seasonal gardening tips. Future content expansion will include premium guides and personalized advice for paid subscribers.
