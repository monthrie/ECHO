# Promotion Strategy

The promotion strategy for the Urban Balcony Gardening Substack will begin with personal networks, including friends and existing social media followers. Future growth plans include potential collaborations with other urban gardening enthusiasts to expand reach. The approach emphasizes authenticity and genuine knowledge sharing rather than aggressive marketing.
