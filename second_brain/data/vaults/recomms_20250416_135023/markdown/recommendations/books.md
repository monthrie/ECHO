# Book Recommendations

A curated collection of recommended books across various genres.

## The Road by Cormac McCarthy

*Post-apocalyptic fiction*

A powerful post-apocalyptic novel with sparse but beautiful writing. Known for its bleak but poetic depiction of a post-apocalyptic world and the relationship between a father and son.

**Related Books:**
- Pachinko by Min Jin Lee
- Dune by Frank Herbert

## Pachinko by Min Jin Lee

*Historical fiction, Family saga, Korean literature*

A captivating multi-generational Korean family saga set in Japan. An epic historical novel following four generations of a Korean family who move to Japan. Explores themes of identity, sacrifice, and resilience.

**Related Books:**
- The Road by Cormac McCarthy
- Dune by Frank Herbert

## Dune by Frank Herbert

*Science fiction, Worldbuilding, Classic*

Features incredible worldbuilding and lives up to its status as a science fiction classic. A landmark of science fiction with complex political, religious, and ecological themes. Features an intricately constructed universe with unique cultural systems.

**Related Books:**
- The Road by Cormac McCarthy
- Pachinko by Min Jin Lee
