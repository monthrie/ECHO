# Ideas for the future

## Human Rights Due Diligence Tool

Human Rights Due Diligence Tool with Dan Murphy could be developed in several ways: helping Dan structure and sell his existing data, or transforming his data into a model for AI fine-tuning. Potential collaboration with CLIM to execute this project.

This idea involves partnering with Dan Murphy to leverage existing data. Consider exploring the fine-tuning approach as it might yield higher value and uniqueness in the market.

## Working with Social Media Influencers

Collaborate with Travis to scale his social media presence (currently 200k Instagram followers) by developing AI agents that function as virtual staff members. This could be developed into a marketable product or business that leverages Travis's audience and promotional capabilities.

This approach could pioneer a new category of AI-powered influencer tools. Consider developing a prototype with Travis to demonstrate efficacy before scaling.

## VITA Development

Develop VITA into the world's first autonomous digital life form, potentially creating an agent arena for testing and demonstration. This initiative could attract funding from early-stage investors in the AI space.

The concept of autonomous digital life forms is cutting-edge and could position the team at the forefront of AI development. The agent arena idea provides a practical testing ground and demonstration platform.

## AI Second Brain Development

Create an AI-powered second brain system, potentially using Obsidian as a front end. This addresses a significant market gap, especially as OpenAI is positioning high-end assistants at $20,000/month. The key differentiator would be the agent framework rather than just the underlying models.

This project requires a compelling proof of concept to attract partners or investors. The integration with existing tools like Obsidian could accelerate adoption and provide immediate user value.

## Follow The Fucking Money

Develop 'Follow The Fucking Money' (FTFM) as an open-source platform that analyzes financial connections. This could be monetized as a Substack publication, operate as a free service using affordable models through Open Router, and allow users to contribute content. The system could visualize data connections similar to Obsidian's popular knowledge maps.

The 'Glenn Green World' approach of analyzing podcasts and revealing inconsistencies could create a compelling public record. The visualization component could be particularly appealing to users accustomed to knowledge graphs.
