# Book Recommendations

A collection of personally recommended books with notes on their distinctive qualities.

## The Road by Cormac McCarthy

A powerful post-apocalyptic novel featuring sparse but beautiful writing that creates a haunting atmosphere.

**Notes:** The sparse prose style creates a haunting atmosphere that perfectly complements the post-apocalyptic setting.

**Related Books:**
- Pachinko by Min Jin Lee
- Dune by Frank Herbert

## Pachinko by Min Jin Lee

A captivating multi-generational saga following a Korean family living in Japan, exploring themes of identity, belonging, and resilience.

**Notes:** This multi-generational story offers deep insights into Korean-Japanese relations and cultural identity.

**Related Books:**
- The Road by Cormac McCarthy
- Dune by Frank Herbert

## Dune by Frank Herbert

Features incredible worldbuilding that has cemented its status as a science fiction classic, with rich political intrigue, ecological themes, and complex characters.

**Notes:** The ecological and political themes remain remarkably relevant despite being written decades ago.

**Related Books:**
- The Road by Cormac McCarthy
- Pachinko by Min Jin Lee
