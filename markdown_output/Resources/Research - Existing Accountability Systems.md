# Research: Existing Systems for Public Accountability in News

## Research Objectives
Investigate current approaches to tracking public statements and accountability in news reporting. This research will support the development of [[FTFM - Public Accountability Information System]] by identifying gaps and opportunities in the existing ecosystem.

## Key Areas of Investigation

### Current Approaches
- Fact-checking organizations (PolitiFact, FactCheck.org, etc.)
- Media monitoring services
- Academic research into statement tracking
- Public records and archives systems

### Wikipedia Limitations
Wikipedia serves well for documenting static information:
- Births, deaths, major events
- Well-established facts
- Historical information

However, it has significant limitations for tracking:
- Day-to-day statements
- Evolving positions
- Accountability for public figures
- Temporal patterns in statements
- Contradictions over time

### Information Verification Challenges
- Methods for verifying the authenticity of statements
- Approaches to classifying and categorizing claims
- Systems for connecting related statements across time
- Techniques for detecting changes in position

### Technical Implementation
- Database structures used for statement tracking
- Entity recognition in news sources
- Relationship mapping between entities and statements
- Search and retrieval techniques for historical statements

## Research Questions
1. What existing systems attempt to track public statements and accountability?
2. What are their limitations and shortcomings?
3. How do they structure and categorize information?
4. What verification methods do they employ?
5. How do they handle the temporal nature of evolving positions?

## Potential Sources
- Academic papers on media accountability
- Documentation from fact-checking organizations
- Interviews with individuals from news accountability projects
- Analysis of Wikipedia's structure and limitations
- Case studies of public figure accountability

## Connection to FTFM
This research will inform the development of FTFM by identifying specific gaps the system should address and potential approaches that have proven effective or ineffective.
