# Open-Source AI Models Research

## Research Objective
Conduct comprehensive research on available open-source AI models that could be leveraged for knowledge management applications.

## Focus Areas
Models with strong capabilities in:
- Text understanding
- Content organization
- Information retrieval
- Semantic comprehension

## Evaluation Criteria
Compare models based on:
- Model size and resource requirements
- Performance metrics on understanding and organization tasks
- Licensing terms
- Community support and maintenance
- Fine-tuning requirements
- Deployment considerations

## Potential Models to Evaluate
- Hugging Face open-source transformers
- LlaMA 2 and derivatives
- Mistral models
- OpenAI's older open-source models
- Specialized knowledge graph models

## Notes
Focus on models with good text understanding and organization capabilities. Consider both large general models that can be fine-tuned and smaller specialized models.
