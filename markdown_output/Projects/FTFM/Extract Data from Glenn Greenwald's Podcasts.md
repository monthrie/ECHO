## Research Request
This project aims to extract transcripts from Glenn Greenwald’s podcasts over the last six months and convert them into structured JSON data.

### Objectives
- **Transcribe Podcasts**: Generate accurate transcripts from audio sources.
- **Extract Named Entities**: Identify key people, organizations, and locations mentioned.
- **Identify Contradictions**: Cross-check statements with past claims.
- **Reference Evidence**: Link relevant sources.

### Timeline & Priority
- **Priority**: Medium
- **Due Date**: July 1, 2024
- **Status**: In Progress
