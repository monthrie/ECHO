## Concept Overview
To enhance accountability in public statements, the ‘Statement Half-Life’ metric tracks how long claims remain unchallenged before being revised or contradicted.

### Implementation
- **Data Collection**: Monitor statements over time.
- **Revision Tracking**: Identify when claims are modified or disproven.
- **Visualization**: Graph changes in claim validity.

### Potential Impact
- Enables tracking of evolving narratives in politics and journalism.
- Helps assess credibility and reliability of sources.
